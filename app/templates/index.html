Which customers do we renew, and on what terms?

Ingrid is an account executive at a fictional wholesaler. Before a customer's yearly contract runs out, she looks at their history and decides what to offer. This small app is where she does that. Finance needs her decisions in the revenue forecast straight away.

Why this needs more than one database

  1. The history is in the lakehouseLast year the company moved to a new ERP, and older orders were not migrated. They exist only in the lakehouse, next to tickets from the support system.
  2. Lakebase serves it to the appSynced tables copy scores, orders and tickets to Postgres, so this page opens in milliseconds.
  3. Ingrid decidesHer decisions go straight into an ordinary Postgres table in Lakebase.
  4. Finance sees it at onceThe forecast in Databricks reads her decisions live, with no pipeline in between.
